## Tips for Usage:
- Offer 3 specific date/time options
- Typical time: 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM (lunch hour)
- Typical duration: 45-60 min (30-40 min presentation, 15-20 min Q&A)
- Mention lunch is provided (people care about food!)
- Be clear about format expectations (in-person vs virtual)
- Provide full address if in-person
- Give audience size so they know scope
