# Follow-Up Email Template

## Variables to Fill:
- `[CONTACT_NAME]` - Contact's first name
- `[SENDER_NAME]` - Joshua German / Elliot Klein / John Simmons
- `[COMPANY_NAME]` - Partner company name
- `[ORIGINAL_TOPIC]` - Topic mentioned in first outreach
- `[NEW_TIMING]` - Updated timeframe suggestion
- `[ADDITIONAL_CONTEXT]` - Any new relevant info (optional)

---

## Template (7-10 days after first outreach)

**Subject:** Re: Partnership Opportunity: Lunch & Learn at TBWA\Chiat\Day LA

Hi [CONTACT_NAME],

I wanted to follow up on my note from last week about bringing [COMPANY_NAME] in for a Lunch & Learn session with our teams at TBWA\Chiat\Day LA.

[OPTIONAL: ADDITIONAL_CONTEXT - e.g., "I saw your recent launch of X feature and it reinforced how valuable this conversation would be for our teams."]

We're looking at [NEW_TIMING] for upcoming sessions, and I think a conversation around [ORIGINAL_TOPIC] would be really timely for our agency.

Let me know if this is something you'd be open to exploring. Happy to jump on a quick call to discuss what this could look like.

Best,
[SENDER_NAME]
[SENDER_TITLE]
TBWA\Chiat\Day LA

## Tips for Usage:
- Send 7-10 days after first outreach if no response
- Keep it brief and non-pushy
- Add new context if available (recent launches, news, etc.)
- Offer flexibility (call, different timing, etc.)
- Restates value without being repetitive
