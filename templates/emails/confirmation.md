# Confirmation Email Template

## Variables to Fill:
- `[CONTACT_NAME]` - Contact's first name
- `[SENDER_NAME]` - Joshua German / Elliot Klein / John Simmons
- `[COMPANY_NAME]` - Partner company name
- `[CONFIRMED_DATE]` - Confirmed date
- `[CONFIRMED_TIME]` - Confirmed time
- `[FORMAT]` - In-person / Virtual
- `[LOCATION]` - Address or Zoom link
- `[TOPIC]` - Confirmed topic/focus
- `[DECK_DEADLINE]` - Deadline for receiving their deck (usually 2-3 days before)

## Tips for Usage:
- Send immediately after date is confirmed
- Be very specific with all logistics
- Set clear deadline for deck review (2-3 days before event)
- Offer to provide context about agency/clients if helpful
- Include your phone number for day-of coordination
- Reassure them you're handling internal logistics
