# First Outreach Email Template

## Variables to Fill:
- `[CONTACT_NAME]` - Contact's first name
- `[SENDER_NAME]` - Joshua German / Elliot Klein / John Simmons
- `[SENDER_TITLE]` - Connections Strategist / Account / Brand Strategist
- `[COMPANY_NAME]` - Partner company name
- `[SPECIFIC_INTEREST]` - Specific reason/interest in their platform/work
- `[RELEVANT_CLIENTS]` - TBWA clients relevant to this vertical (optional)
- `[SUGGESTED_TOPIC]` - Suggested topic for the Lunch & Learn
- `[PROPOSED_TIMING]` - Suggested timeframe (e.g., "later this month" or "early March")

---

## Template

**Subject:** Partnership Opportunity: Lunch & Learn at TBWA\Chiat\Day LA

Hi [CONTACT_NAME],

I'm [SENDER_NAME], [SENDER_TITLE] here at TBWA\Chiat\Day LA. We're the agency behind brands like [RELEVANT_CLIENTS - examples: Gatorade, Airbnb, Nissan], and we're always looking to deepen our understanding of the evolving media and technology landscape.

I've been following [COMPANY_NAME]'s work, particularly [SPECIFIC_INTEREST], and think your team's perspective would be incredibly valuable for our agency.

We run a regular Lunch & Learn series where we bring in industry leaders to share insights with our teams across media, strategy, and creative. Past sessions with partners like Genius, Pinterest, and Outfront Media have been hugely successful in building relationships and keeping our teams ahead of the curve.

I'd love to explore bringing someone from [COMPANY_NAME] in to talk about [SUGGESTED_TOPIC]. It's a great opportunity to share your platform's capabilities with our teams, and it's a win-win for building our partnership.

Would you or someone on your team be interested in joining us for a session [PROPOSED_TIMING]? We're flexible on format and can work around your schedule.

Looking forward to connecting.

Best,
[SENDER_NAME]
[SENDER_TITLE]
TBWA\Chiat\Day LA

## Example (Filled In)

**Subject:** Partnership Opportunity: Lunch & Learn at TBWA\Chiat\Day LA

Hi Sarah,

I'm Joshua German, Connections Strategist here at TBWA\Chiat\Day LA. We're the agency behind brands like Gatorade, Airbnb, and Nissan, and we're always looking to deepen our understanding of the evolving media and technology landscape.

I've been following OpenAI's recent announcement around launching ads on your platform, and think your team's perspective on this new advertising opportunity would be incredibly valuable for our agency.

We run a regular Lunch & Learn series where we bring in industry leaders to share insights with our teams across media, strategy, and creative. Past sessions with partners like Genius, Pinterest, and Outfront Media have been hugely successful in building relationships and keeping our teams ahead of the curve.

I'd love to explore bringing someone from OpenAI in to talk about your advertising platform and how brands can leverage AI in their marketing strategies. It's a great opportunity to share your platform's capabilities with our teams, and it's a win-win for building our partnership.

Would you or someone on your team be interested in joining us for a session later this month? We're flexible on format and can work around your schedule.

Looking forward to connecting.

Best,
Joshua German
Connections Strategist
TBWA\Chiat\Day LA

---

## Tips for Usage:
- Keep tone professional but warm
- Make specific reference to their recent work/launches to show you've done research
- Emphasize win-win (not asking for favor, offering partnership opportunity)
- Be flexible on timing and format
- Name-drop successful past L&Ls to build credibility
- Mention relevant TBWA clients to show alignment
