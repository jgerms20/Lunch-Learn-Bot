# Thank You & Recap Email Template

## Variables to Fill:
- `[CONTACT_NAME]` - Contact's first name
- `[SENDER_NAME]` - Joshua German / Elliot Klein / John Simmons
- `[COMPANY_NAME]` - Partner company name
- `[EVENT_DATE]` - Date of the Lunch & Learn
- `[KEY_TAKEAWAY_1]` - First major takeaway
- `[KEY_TAKEAWAY_2]` - Second major takeaway
- `[KEY_TAKEAWAY_3]` - Third major takeaway
- `[SPECIFIC_HIGHLIGHT]` - Specific moment/question that resonated
- `[FUTURE_OPPORTUNITY]` - Optional: mention of staying in touch for future collaboration

---

Hi Sarah,

- How brands can leverage OpenAI's advertising platform for targeted campaigns
- The importance of thoughtful AI integration vs. AI for AI's sake

The conversation around how different industries are approaching AI advertising sparked a lot of great questions, and our media and strategy teams are already thinking about how to apply these insights to upcoming client pitches.

We really appreciate you taking the time to share your expertise with us. I'd love to keep the conversation going and explore opportunities to work together as relevant projects come up.

Thanks again, and looking forward to staying connected.

Best,
Joshua German
Connections Strategist
TBWA\Chiat\Day LA

---

## Tips for Usage:
- Send within 24-48 hours of the event
- Be specific about what resonated (shows you were engaged)
- Highlight concrete takeaways (not just generic praise)
- Mention team's enthusiasm to validate their time investment
- Keep door open for future collaboration
- Keep it concise but genuine
