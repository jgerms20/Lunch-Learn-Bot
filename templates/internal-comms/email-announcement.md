# Internal Email Announcement Template (1 Week Out)

## Variables to Fill:
- `[COMPANY_NAME]` - Partner company presenting
- `[SPEAKER_NAME]` - Speaker's full name
- `[SPEAKER_TITLE]` - Speaker's title
- `[TOPIC]` - Main topic/focus
- `[DATE]` - Event date
- `[TIME]` - Event time
- `[LOCATION]` - Room/location
- `[WHY_IT_MATTERS]` - 2-3 sentences on relevance to our work
- `[FOOD]` - What food is being provided
- `[RSVP_REQUIRED]` - Yes/No, and method if yes

---

## Template (All-Agency)

**Subject:** Lunch & Learn: [COMPANY_NAME] on [TOPIC] - [DATE]

Hey team,

We're excited to announce our next Lunch & Learn session:

**WHO:** [SPEAKER_NAME], [SPEAKER_TITLE] at [COMPANY_NAME]

**WHAT:** [TOPIC]

**WHEN:** [DATE] at [TIME]

**WHERE:** [LOCATION]

**WHY YOU SHOULD CARE:**
[WHY_IT_MATTERS]

**FOOD:** [FOOD]

[IF RSVP_REQUIRED: Please RSVP by [DEADLINE] so we can get an accurate headcount for food.]
[IF NOT REQUIRED: No RSVP needed - just show up!]

Mark your calendars and come ready with questions. This is a great opportunity to learn from one of the leading [VERTICAL] platforms and think about how we can apply these insights to our client work.

See you there!

[SENDER_NAME]
Connections Strategy

## Tips for Usage:
- Send 1 week before event
- Keep subject line clear and scannable
- Always mention the food (people care!)
- Make "why you should care" specific and concrete
- For department-specific sends, tailor the relevance
- Include calendar invite with email
- Keep tone energetic but professional
