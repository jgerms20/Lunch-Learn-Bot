# Internal Email Reminder Template (Day Of)

## Variables to Fill:
- `[COMPANY_NAME]` - Partner company presenting
- `[TIME]` - Event time
- `[LOCATION]` - Room/location
- `[FOOD]` - What food is being provided

---

## Template

**Subject:** Today: [COMPANY_NAME] Lunch & Learn at [TIME]

Happening in a few hours!

**[COMPANY_NAME] Lunch & Learn**
**[TIME]** | **[LOCATION]**
**[FOOD]** will be served

See you there.

---

## Example (Filled In)

**Subject:** Today: OpenAI Lunch & Learn at 12 PM

Happening in a few hours!

**OpenAI Lunch & Learn**
**12:00 PM** | **Main Conference Room**
**Chipotle** will be served

See you there.

---

## Tips for Usage:
- Send morning of event (9-10 AM ideal)
- Ultra concise (people are scanning emails fast)
- Just company, time, location, food
- Keep subject line simple and clear
