# Teams Message Templates

## Variables to Fill:
- `[COMPANY_NAME]` - Partner company presenting
- `[TOPIC_SHORT]` - Topic in 3-5 words
- `[DATE_TIME]` - When it's happening
- `[WHY_CARE_ONE_LINER]` - One sentence on why it matters
- `[FOOD]` - What food is being provided
- `[LOCATION]` - Room/location

---

## Template (Initial Announcement - 1 Week Out)

Hey team!

**[COMPANY_NAME]** is coming in **[DATE_TIME]** to talk about **[TOPIC_SHORT]**

[WHY_CARE_ONE_LINER]

**Food:** [FOOD]
**Where:** [LOCATION]

See you there!

---

## Example (Initial Announcement)

Hey team!

**OpenAI** is coming in **Tuesday, March 5th at 12 PM** to talk about **AI-powered advertising**

First look at their new ad platform and how we can leverage AI for client work.

**Food:** Chipotle
**Where:** Main Conference Room

See you there!

## Tips for Usage:
- Keep it casual and hype-building
- No emojis (per user preference)
- Short and punchy
- Food always gets mentioned (it matters!)
- Can be even more concise than email
- Post in relevant Teams channels (General, Media, Strategy, etc.)
