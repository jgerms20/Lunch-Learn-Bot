# Internal Email Reminder Template (Day Before)

## Variables to Fill:
- `[COMPANY_NAME]` - Partner company presenting
- `[SPEAKER_NAME]` - Speaker's name
- `[TOPIC]` - Main topic (shortened)
- `[TIME]` - Event time
- `[LOCATION]` - Room/location
- `[FOOD]` - What food is being provided

---

## Template

**Subject:** Tomorrow: [COMPANY_NAME] Lunch & Learn at [TIME]

Quick reminder!

Our Lunch & Learn with [SPEAKER_NAME] from [COMPANY_NAME] is tomorrow:

**WHAT:** [TOPIC]
**WHEN:** Tomorrow at [TIME]
**WHERE:** [LOCATION]
**FOOD:** [FOOD]

See you there!

---

## Example (Filled In)

**Subject:** Tomorrow: OpenAI Lunch & Learn at 12 PM

Quick reminder!

Our Lunch & Learn with Sarah Chen from OpenAI is tomorrow:

**WHAT:** AI-Powered Advertising
**WHEN:** Tomorrow (Tuesday, March 5th) at 12:00 PM
**WHERE:** Main Conference Room
**FOOD:** Chipotle

See you there!

---

## Tips for Usage:
- Send day before, ideally in morning
- Keep it super short (people are busy)
- Just the essentials: what, when, where, food
- No need to re-explain why it matters (they already know from first email)
