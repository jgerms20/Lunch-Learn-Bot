# Skill: Draft Partner Outreach Email

## Purpose
Generate a professional, warm outreach email to invite a media partner to present at a TBWA\Chiat\Day LA Lunch & Learn session.

## How to Use This Skill

### Trigger Phrase
"Draft an outreach email for [COMPANY] about [TOPIC]"

### Required Information
Provide Claude with:
1. **Company Name** - Who you're reaching out to
2. **Contact Name** - First name of the person (if known, otherwise use "there")
3. **Topic/Focus** - What you want them to present about
4. **Sender** - Joshua German / Elliot Klein / John Simmons
5. **Specific Interest** - Why this partner specifically? (Recent launch, platform feature, cultural relevance)
6. **Relevant Clients** (Optional) - Which TBWA clients are relevant to this vertical

### Example Usage

**User:**
"Draft an outreach email for OpenAI about their new advertising platform. The contact is Sarah Chen. This is from Joshua. Specific interest is their recent announcement about launching ads. Relevant clients: Gatorade, Nissan, Airbnb."

**Claude Will:**
1. Use the first outreach template from `templates/emails/first-outreach.md`
2. Fill in all variables with the provided information
3. Adjust tone to be professional but warm
4. Include specific reference to the recent ads announcement
5. Mention relevant TBWA clients
6. Suggest flexible timing
7. Emphasize win-win partnership opportunity
8. Output ready-to-send email

## Template Reference
The email should follow this structure:
- Subject line that's professional and clear
- Personal intro (Hi [Name], I'm [Sender], [Title] at TBWA\Chiat\Day LA)
- Agency context (brands we work with)
- Specific interest in their work/platform
- Lunch & Learn invitation
- Flexible scheduling
- Signature

## Tone Guidelines
- Professional but warm (not overly formal)
- Show genuine interest in their work
- Emphasize mutual benefit (not asking for a favor)
- Be specific about why they're relevant
- Keep it concise (3-4 short paragraphs)

## Output Format
Return a complete, ready-to-send email with:
- Subject line
- Full email body
- Proper formatting
- Signature block

## Quality Checklist
Before sending, verify:
- [ ] Contact name is spelled correctly
- [ ] Company name is accurate
- [ ] Specific interest is compelling and timely
- [ ] Relevant TBWA clients are mentioned (if applicable)
- [ ] Tone is warm but professional
- [ ] No typos or grammatical errors
- [ ] Sender information is correct
- [ ] Call to action is clear (explore bringing someone in)

## Common Variations

### No specific contact yet
If you don't have a contact name, use "Hi there" or "Hello" instead of personalizing

### Different senders
- **Joshua German** - Connections Strategist (most common)
- **Elliot Klein** - Account
- **John Simmons** - Brand Strategist

### Follow-up email
If this is a follow-up (7-10 days after first outreach), use the follow-up template instead
