# Skill: Research Contact at Company

## Purpose
Find the right contact person at a media partner company for Lunch & Learn outreach, using a systematic research approach.

## How to Use This Skill

### Trigger Phrase
"Research contact at [COMPANY]" or "Find contact for [COMPANY] in [VERTICAL]"

### Required Information
Provide Claude with:
1. **Company Name** - Full official name
2. **Vertical/Focus** - What area (e.g., social media, streaming, AI, OOH)
3. **Geography Preference** - LA-based preferred, but flexible

### Optional Information
- **Specific Department** - Partnerships, marketing, agency sales, etc.
- **Internal Connection** - Anyone at TBWA who might know someone there
- **Context** - Why we want to bring them in (helps find right person)

### Example Usage

**User:**
"Research contact at OpenAI for their advertising platform. Vertical is AI/Emerging Tech. We want someone from partnerships or marketing who can speak to their new ads product."

**Claude Will:**
1. Ask if there's an internal TBWA connection first
2. Outline research strategy (LinkedIn, company website, press releases)
3. Suggest target titles/roles to look for
4. Provide search approach and keywords
5. Document findings in structured format
6. Recommend confidence level (high/medium/low)

## Research Process

### Step 1: Check Internal Sources First
**Always start here**
- Ask: "Does anyone at TBWA know someone at [COMPANY]?"
- Check if we've worked with them on client projects
- Look for past L&L contacts in our database

### Step 2: Identify Target Role
**What role/title makes sense?**

For different verticals:
- **Social Media/Streaming/OOH**: Agency Partnerships, Agency Sales, Partner Manager
- **AI/Tech Platforms**: Business Development, Partnerships, Marketing
- **Cultural/Trend Partners**: Marketing, Brand Partnerships, Community
- **Podcast/Audio**: Sales, Agency Partnerships, Brand Partnerships

Target level:
- **Ideal**: Manager/Director level (hands-on, accessible)
- **Backup**: VP level (decision maker, may delegate)
- **Avoid**: C-suite (unless very small company or personal connection)

### Step 3: LinkedIn Research
**Search Strategy:**

1. **Basic Search**
   - Go to LinkedIn
   - Search: "[COMPANY] partnerships" or "[COMPANY] agency sales"
   - Filter by current company

2. **Title Search**
   - Look for: "Partner Manager", "Agency Partnerships", "Business Development"
   - Geographic filter: Los Angeles (preferred)
   - Check profiles for relevant experience

3. **What to Look For**
   - Are they client-facing?
   - Do they mention working with agencies/brands?
   - Recent activity about L&Ls or speaking engagements?
   - Based in LA or willing to travel?

### Step 4: Company Website
**Research Path:**
1. Go to company website
2. Check "About" → "Team" or "Leadership"
3. Look for press releases about partnerships or advertising
4. Check blog posts for author names (who writes about partnerships?)
5. Look for "Contact" or "Partnerships" page

### Step 5: Google Search
**Effective Searches:**
- "[COMPANY] agency partnerships contact"
- "[COMPANY] advertising sales team"
- "[COMPANY] speaking events" (who represents them?)
- "[PERSON NAME] [COMPANY] email" (if you found a name)

### Step 6: Email Discovery
**Finding Email Addresses:**

Common patterns for most companies:
- firstname.lastname@company.com
- firstname@company.com
- flastname@company.com

Tools that can help:
- Hunter.io (email pattern finder)
- LinkedIn (sometimes shows email)
- Company website team pages

**Don't guess** - Only use if you're confident in the pattern

## Output Format

Return findings in this structure:

```
=== CONTACT RESEARCH: [COMPANY] ===

INTERNAL SOURCES:
- [Any TBWA connections? Yes/No + details]

RECOMMENDED CONTACT:
Name: [Full name or "TBD - Research needed"]
Title: [Exact title]
Email: [Email if found, or "Not found - suggest calling company"]
Phone: [If available]
LinkedIn: [Profile URL]
Location: [City, State]

CONFIDENCE LEVEL: [High / Medium / Low]
- High = Direct contact confirmed with email
- Medium = Right person identified, email needs verification
- Low = Best guess based on title, needs validation

REASONING:
[Why this is the right person - their role, responsibilities, relevant experience]

SOURCE TRAIL:
[How you found them - LinkedIn search, company website, press release, etc.]

BACKUP CONTACTS:
[1-2 alternative people if primary doesn't respond]

NEXT STEPS:
[What to do with this information - verify email, call company, send outreach, etc.]
```

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Checked for internal TBWA connections first
- [ ] Target role makes sense for the vertical
- [ ] LinkedIn profile confirms current role
- [ ] Email pattern verified (if email found)
- [ ] Location noted (LA preference documented)
- [ ] Confidence level is honest and justified
- [ ] Backup contacts identified
- [ ] Source trail is clear (reproducible research)

## Best Practices
- **Always check internal first** - warm intros are better
- **LA-based is ideal** - in-person L&Ls are more engaging
- **Go for doers, not just decision makers** - Directors/Managers often more responsive
- **Document everything** - you might need to retrace steps
- **Don't guess emails** - better to call company and ask
- **Be transparent about confidence** - "Medium" is okay if research is solid

## Common Challenges

### Can't find anyone on LinkedIn
- Try company website team page
- Search for press releases or blog authors
- Call company main line and ask for partnerships team

### Multiple potential contacts
- Go with most recent/active on LinkedIn
- Prefer LA-based over others
- Choose title that's client-facing

### Email pattern unclear
- Try LinkedIn InMail first
- Call company and ask to be connected
- Use general partnerships email

### Person left the company
- Check their LinkedIn for current role
- Search for their replacement
- Use general company email
